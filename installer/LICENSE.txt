3. HOW IT WORKS

   Varnaakshara uses a system-wide keyboard hook to intercept
   your keystrokes and convert them into Indian script characters
   in real time. This is how all transliteration IMEs work.

   Your keystrokes are processed LOCALLY on your computer.
   Nothing is sent to any server. No typing data is collected,
   stored, or transmitted.
